**Full description (max 4000):**
FocusFlow helps you work in focused sprints with short breaks in between (25 / 5 by default, fully adjustable).

• Beautiful dial timer with focus, short break and long break phases
• Calm focus sounds (rain, wind, deep focus, white noise) that keep playing with the screen off
• Private statistics: today, last 7 days, total time and day streak
• 12 achievements to keep you motivated
• Gentle reminder if you leave the app during a focus session (it never reads your other apps)
• Light, dark and Material You colors; English and Arabic (RTL)
• 100% offline: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your data never leaves your phone, and you can delete it any time.
